# func_002035B0 (loaders.c, texture upload register block)

592 against retail 600. Written from retail; the Lombyte FUN_00202d78 draft has wrong clamps.

The logic is certain: lo/hi = max(size>>7, 1) and max(size>>6, 1); the (field + g) sums and `w` are computed before the mode branch; the words are a TEX0-style and a MIPTBP1-style value.

What is left: retail copies each int operand (`daddu`) before shifting it into the 64-bit words, and spills a4 to sp+0; ours shifts the saved registers directly, which is 2 instructions shorter. SCE_GS-macro spellings with u_long casts and inline helpers made no difference.
