# func_0011B4C8 (sceSifCallRpc) — 4/492; not committed

Lombyte (MIT) matches it (src/sdk/rpc/sce_sif_call_rpc.c) with an empty asm
memory barrier after `arg0->unk0 = packet`, which our rules forbid. Without
it (build-sn/try/crpc_best.c) the only difference is the order of the two
stores to +0x0 and +0x4 of the client: the scheduler puts +0x4 first
whatever the statement order. Loading unk18 in the store, a pointer-typed
unk0 and -fno-schedule-insns are all worse.
