# func_00228690 (pause.c, strip packet, sibling of func_002284E8)

Right size (460), 136 bytes off, from Lombyte's FUN_00227378 draft (MIT).

Two fixes to the draft:
- The second strip store is at the advanced pointer's offset 0, not `unk8`.
- The header is written as `((long *)p)[1..9]` relative to the advanced pointer; the draft's Tag fields are 0x10 off.

What is left: retail sets `count = 0` in t7 among the header stores and keeps `p` in `$v0`; ours sets count in the preheader and keeps p in t7. Initialising count before the `if` needs a third saved register (464 bytes). See also the comment above the stub in pause.c.
