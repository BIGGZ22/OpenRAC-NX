# func_0022CA00 (skyfunc.c, SkyDrawShellTextured)

Near-miss: 58/572, right size.

- The array is a VLA, `unsigned char vis[sh->count]`.
- func_0022D2AC takes 3 args: the handwritten routine never reads `$a3`.
- `more = next < sh->count` has to be computed before the D_00160510 toggle. The toggle is a cast store through the short SDA declaration, so the scheduler cannot move the count load above it.
- The D_0022D520 pointer args are precomputed before the D2AC call.

What is left is local-alloc register choice in two places:
- the tile-address copy (`daddu v0,v1`) in the next-tile func_0020C210 call;
- the c/b/a temporaries before func_0022D2AC (ours t0/a3/v0, retail a3/v0/t0).

Tried without effect: a 12-bit bitfield for tbp, swapping pb/pc, a `buf` local, and indexing by `vis[i]`.
