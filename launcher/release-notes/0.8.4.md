## The game

- **The Suck Cannon no longer crashes the game.** On Eudora, a circular saw robot drawn all the way into the cannon stopped the game, on both discs. For several kinds of creature the cannon ran the wrong step: it grabbed the creature again when it should have placed it in the muzzle, and the next frame read through a bad target. The same mix-up affected creatures on most planets. Sucking up and firing creatures now works as on the console, and they home in on the locked target.
- **Gaspar's Pilot's Helmet no longer crashes the game.** Lava splashes from the falling rocks can fill the game's room for objects; the original game does this too. If you picked up the helmet at that moment, its scene could not create one of its actors and the game stopped. Now the scene plays without that actor, and the log notes it. A proper fix, more room for objects on every planet, is planned.
- **Nanotech crates keep their glow at the sides of a wide picture.** In 16:9 the orb above a crate went out when the crate was near the left or right edge. The orb's own visibility test still used the 4:3 picture.
- rac1-decomp and the NTSC decomp are synced.

## Differences from the original

- **A new section on each game's page in the launcher** lists what OpenRAC does differently from the PlayStation 2 game, and why. Changes the game logic doesn't depend on can be switched back to the original, for players who want the console's behaviour exactly. For now that is the three wide-screen fixes: effects, nanotech orbs, and seas, lava and sand at the sides of a wide picture. The game's Options page has the same switches under **Game fixes**. Crash guards such as the helmet's are listed but always on, since the original behaviour would only crash on a PC.

## Crash reports

- A crash report now says which object the game was updating, its class and position, and where the camera was, so a crash can be placed exactly. Reports from the US disc now include Ratchet's position too.
- Warnings from the game's own code reach the log you send with a report.

The installers are built by the launcher workflow and are not signed for Windows or macOS. Your levels are not extracted again for this version.
