## The game

- **The Suck Cannon no longer crashes the game.** On Eudora, a circular saw robot drawn all the way into the cannon stopped the game, on both discs. The cannon ran the wrong step for creatures of a few kinds: it grabbed a creature again when it should have placed it in the muzzle, and the next frame read through the bad target. The same mix-up affected creatures on most planets, so sucking up and firing creatures now works as on the console, homing in on the locked target.

The installers are built by the launcher workflow and are not signed for Windows or macOS. Your levels are not extracted again for this version.
