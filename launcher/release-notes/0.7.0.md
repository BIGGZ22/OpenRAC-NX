## The game

- **The US disc plays.** The launcher sets up and plays the US version of Ratchet & Clank from its own disc. Pause menus, in-game scenes, menu text, the liquid strips, collision and memory card reads all work; the HUD edges, Video rows and Planets page match the European game.
- **Games have their own names.** Each game is named by its title and version, and on macOS the game is a proper app with an Apple-shaped icon.
- **Fixes.** A press right after picking a planet no longer leaves a black screen. Memory card reads finish on the US game.

## The launcher

- **One path for every disc.** Players insert any valid disc and the same actions work; no region is ever shown.
- **A save editor** on the Play page. It reads and checks Ratchet & Clank saves, can move a save to another planet, shows the skill points by name, and has an advanced mode for raw chunks. Restores are guarded, and memory cards are backed up by their folder.
- **Modding for whichever disc you set up.** The editor reads both discs, exports and imports models and textures from either, and packs edited levels for either; Godot projects and packed levels sit in their version's folder.
- **Levels in a folder per version.** One levels root, a folder for each version; an export made earlier moves into its own folder.
- **A macOS icon** in Apple's shape, and game tiles that keep their still picture and zoom slightly on hover.
- **An option to stop looking for updates** at start.

## For contributors

- The European game's host work since 2026-10-13 is carried to the US game, and hostgen resolves level places, level copies of executable functions and callee typedefs more precisely.
- The Flatpak packaging files were removed.

The installers are built by the launcher workflow and are not signed for Windows or macOS.
