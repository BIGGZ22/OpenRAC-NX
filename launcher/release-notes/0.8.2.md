## The game

- **The title screen** draws its world again. Two of the game's vector routines came in from the decompilation as console instructions, which do nothing on a computer; the port has its own versions of them now (European disc).
- **Ratchet's shadow.** Ratchet casts the game's shadow on the ground beneath him, shaped by his animation, on both discs.
- **Water and windows.**
  - Umbris: the sea no longer covers the glow and the see-through parts of what stands in front of it. Seas and lava are drawn where the game draws them, after the scenery and before the characters.
  - Novalis: the windows by the plumber are glass again, not solid panes. See-through texels of the scenery blend, as on the console.
- **Pokitaru's sky** is the sky again instead of flat cyan. Two far-off objects the game never draws were drawn over it.
- **Wide screens.** The nanotech crates and other objects at the left and right edges of a 16:9 picture keep their effects.
- **Music** fades from one track into the next instead of cutting.
- **The Trespasser's lock rings** draw properly again, on both discs.
- **Vulkan (experimental).** Fixes for corrupted pictures on some Windows drivers (AMD, on Veldin and Novalis).

## The launcher

- **Mods in an installed launcher.** The Mods tab, the level editor and Play with mods were in a checkout's launcher only; the installed launcher has them too.

## For contributors

- hostgen keeps the statics of different candidates apart, and a declaration the definition supersedes takes the definition's prototype.
- Windows diagnostic reports and regression runs work.
- rac1-decomp and the NTSC decomp are synced.

The installers are built by the launcher workflow and are not signed for Windows or macOS. Your levels are not extracted again for this version.
