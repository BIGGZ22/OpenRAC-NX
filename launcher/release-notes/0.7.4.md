## The game

- **No more crash after finishing Veldin on Windows.** The cutscene leading into Novalis stopped the game on Windows: one of the game's calls handed its numbers over in an order Windows reads differently. Every call like it is fixed.
- **The US disc behaves much more like the original.** A pass over the code every US planet runs, checked against the US game itself, fixed about 25 places that still used the PAL version's values: sine and cosine, the 2D drawing that the HUD and menus go through, the depth buffer, menu text, a music cue, the pause menu's slide-in, the map, and water ripples on some planets. Expect a steadier HUD and vendor screens on the US disc.
- **Every remaining piece of the PAL game the port runs has now been checked** against the original, with a few last fixes (the closest-point helper used for collisions, a disc command handler).

## The launcher

- **Progress you can read.** While the launcher builds the game or reads your disc, a strip under the game shows what it is doing in plain words, with a progress bar when it can tell how far along it is. The Play button stays put. The raw output is under **Details**.
- **Crash reports you can tidy up.** Dismiss the crash notice (and the "crashed recently" marks), and delete single logs and crash reports, or all of them, from the game's page.

The installers are built by the launcher workflow and are not signed. Your levels are not extracted again for this version.
