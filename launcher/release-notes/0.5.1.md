## The launcher

- **The launcher updates itself.** When a new release is out it can download and install it from inside the app.
- **One button from a set-up disc to the game.** Once a disc is set up, a single button takes you the rest of the way.

## The game

- **Cutscenes.** In-game cutscenes start, and chained cutscenes play back to back.
- **Ratchet no longer freezes after the wrench throw**, and a thrown wrench leaves his hand.
- **Translucent and glowing objects blend properly**, including the nanotech cluster's sphere, halo and glass sheen.
- **Particles** take their textures from the level's own table, so they no longer show the wrong picture.
- **The flight between planets** shows the planet and the moving stars.
- **No flash when the pause menu closes**, and its picture fills the 60 Hz frame.

## For contributors

- The European decompilation was synced again, and hostgen copes with attribute macros and local externs in stack frames.

The installers are built by the launcher workflow and are not signed for Windows or macOS.
