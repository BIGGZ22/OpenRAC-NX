## The launcher

- **Set a game up from your own disc.** Point the launcher at your PlayStation 2 disc image, as OpenGOAL does: it reads the game's data from your copy, prepares the levels, and Play starts the game. No emulator is involved anywhere; the game runs as a native program.
- **One page per game, one main button.** Each game has a tile, and its page has the button for the next step (set up, then Play). Details for developers sit behind Developer tools.
- **Compact or Classic layout.** A new Compact layout is the default, and the original look stays available in Settings.
- **Save files.** The page shows your save slots with their bolts, planet and play time, and backs up and restores memory cards.
- **Discord.** Your status says what you are doing ("Exploring Kerwan"), with the planet, your bolts and the time you have spent there.
- **Mods page.** Open one level in the Godot editor, edit its mobys, ties and shrubs, pack the edits back into the game's data and play with them.
- **Installers** are built for Windows, Linux and macOS. The window resizes, pages never scroll sideways and elapsed time keeps counting.

## The game

- **Ratchet & Clank (European disc) plays in a native window.** It now has sound effects, music and speech streamed from the disc, and sharp text at any size.
- **Display settings** on the game's Video page: picture shape (including wider than 4:3, with the HUD kept at the sides), frame rate and full screen. The graphics card is named and checked, and frames can use multisampling and sharper textures.
- **A volume setting** that reaches silence.
- **Fixes that you would notice.**
  - The game keeps running while its window is dragged.
  - Ratchet stands on a crate he lands on, crates break, and enemies' hits and knockback work.
  - The camera follows Ratchet through a level, because a level's program is read from the disc image.
  - No blue strip as a story card ends, and no HUD flicker when its textures are repacked.
  - Objects that turn fast still move smoothly between ticks, and deleted objects are no longer drawn.
  - The keyboard counts only while the game's window has the focus.
- **For debugging:** a debug overlay, a frame dump, pause, single-step and slow motion.

## Where the port stands

- The first work on the US (NTSC-U) disc: the renderers' addresses and the lists, host code and backfilled C carried over from the European game. It is not playable yet.

## For contributors

- The editor can translate a moby class's update function into GDScript, with a C parser and a helper table behind it (docs say what is still needed).
- hostgen reads decompilations that name functions `FUN_` and keep drafts, and gained a number of fixes for stack locals and casts.
- `versionmap` finds a version's addresses in another version.

The installers are built by the launcher workflow and are not signed for Windows or macOS.
