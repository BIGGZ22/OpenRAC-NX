## The launcher

- **Play waits until the levels are whole.** Some players saw a black world on Veldin: the levels read from the disc were missing or incomplete and nothing said so. Now the game checks all 21 levels itself, without a window, and names any it cannot draw and why. Play is offered only when every level passes.
- **Broken levels are read again.** If a check fails, the launcher reads the failing levels from your disc once more, in place of the old ones, and checks again.
- **From an earlier install**, the main button says "Check the levels" once; it takes a few seconds when everything is fine.

## The game

- A level whose scenery cannot be read now says so in the picture, and what to do, instead of showing a black world.

The installers are built by the launcher workflow and are not signed for Windows or macOS.
