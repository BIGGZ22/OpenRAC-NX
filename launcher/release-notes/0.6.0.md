## The game

- **Shiny things shine.** Metal, chrome and glass on objects are drawn the way the game does it, from the data your disc holds. Parts of textures that are only partly see-through blend properly, cutout textures are sharper, and glows draw their partly transparent pixels.
- **Ratchet holds his weapon properly.** His arms take the stance of the weapon in his hands. The Blaster and the Pyrocitor, whose model had a joint that collapsed, are kept.
- **The Gadgetron vendor looks right.** Its hologram, item model and salesman draw; its screens go over the vendor rather than under it; and it opens without an unfaded frame.
- **Menus.** The pause pages show Ratchet and the items in their panels, and Clank stays behind Ratchet on the Gadgets page. Panels the game draws off screen now appear.
- **Start skips** the flight between planets and in-game cutscenes.
- **Objects the game never draws are no longer drawn**, and the joints of posed objects follow the game's modifiers.

## The launcher

- **Models and textures on the Mods page.** Export a level's models and textures, edit them in your own tools, and import them back. Overrides packs hold your edits, the game reads a mod's textures and meshes first, and the page manages them.
- **The launcher's version** is shown in the header.
- **Update window** has a full-bleed backdrop and restarts reliably.
