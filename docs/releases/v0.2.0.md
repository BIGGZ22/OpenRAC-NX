# OpenRAC-NX v0.2.0

Play **Ratchet & Clank (2002)** on Nintendo Switch with native deko3d rendering, a redesigned launcher, and more display and gameplay options.

## ✨ What's new

### 🚀 Rendering & performance

- Native **deko3d** rendering for both the game and launcher.
- Reduced animation allocation overhead through reused character-pose memory.
- Updated frame pacing to reduce held-back or skipped pictures after slow updates.
- Correct PAL game speed through the game's 50/60 Hz timing changes.
- Optional **Status Monitor FPS and resolution reporting**.

### 🎨 Launcher & customization

- Redesigned launcher with controller and touch navigation.
- Custom game artwork, menu colours, and light/dark themes.
- Resolution presets starting at **480p**, with separate handheld and docked profiles.
- Saved display, audio, gameplay, and controller settings.

### 🎮 Gameplay & fixes

- Lock-strafe toggle, experimental FOV adjustment, and controller rebinding.
- Fixed Gadgetron lettering, the Pyrocitor shop preview, and fade-edge lines.
- Selected upstream fixes, including the PAL Quartu-flight call.
- Optional raised clock profile, **off by default**, with restore requests on HOME, sleep, and exit. External clock managers may override these requests.
- Diagnostic counters now turn off properly at `0`.

## 📥 First-time installation

**You need your own Ratchet & Clank PAL Europe/Australia v2.00 ISO (`SCES_509.16`). Game data is not supplied.**

1. Download `openrac-rac1-pal-deko3d-v0.2.0.nro` below.
2. Put the NRO and your supported ISO in `SD:/switch/openrac/`. Keep only one ISO in that folder.
3. Leave at least **2 GiB additional free space** for setup.
4. Launch the NRO in **full application mode** through Homebrew Menu or a compatible loader.
5. Let Setup prepare the game, then select **Play**.

Keep the ISO on the SD card after setup. You can rename the NRO to `openrac-rac1-pal.nro`.

## 🔄 Updating an existing installation

1. **Back up your saves:** `SD:/switch/openrac/memcard/`.
2. Replace the old NRO with this download. If its filename differs, remove the old NRO so you launch the right build.
3. Keep your ISO, prepared data, settings, and saves in place.

You do not need a test `env.txt` for normal play. `SHA256SUMS.txt` is provided to verify the download.

## ⚠️ Performance & known issues

An **overclock and full application mode** are required for playable performance in demanding scenes. Performance varies by area; a steady 60 FPS is not guaranteed.

The latest pacing and clock-profile changes still need console verification. Cutscene animation/audio timing and performance consistency remain under investigation, and a complete playthrough has not been verified.

## 🐛 Report a problem

[Open a GitHub issue](https://github.com/BIGGZ22/OpenRAC-NX/issues/new) with your game version, planet/scene, what happened, and how to reproduce it. Include your handheld/docked mode, resolution, clock settings, and a clip if possible.

Game log: `SD:/switch/openrac/openrac-rac1-pal.log`.
